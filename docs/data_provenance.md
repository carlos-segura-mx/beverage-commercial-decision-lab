# Synthetic data and assumptions

[English README](../README.md) | [README en español](README.es.md)

The entire beverage commercial dataset is generated by `src/build_analysis.py`, with random seed `20260831`. No private client data or third-party beverage prices are included. The model covers 104 simulated weeks starting 2024-09-02, six synthetic beverage products and 60 simulated points of sale.

## Dataset contract

| Source | Grain | Nature | Used for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `data/fact_sales.csv` | Week x point of sale x SKU | Synthetic transactions | Historical sell-out and contribution |
| `data/dim_product.csv` | SKU | Synthetic master data | Product labels, cost and parameter context |
| `data/dim_customer.csv` | Point of sale | Synthetic master data | Channel and zone descriptions |
| `data/dim_date.csv` | Simulated weekly date | Synthetic calendar | Weekly series |
| `data/forecast_metrics.csv` | SKU x method | Derived from synthetic series | Historical forecast evaluation |
| `data/forecast_future.csv` | Future simulated week x SKU | Derived from synthetic series | Historical projection example |
| `data/pricing_model_inputs.json` | SKU plus elasticity matrix | Derived and assumed | Interactive discount simulations |
| `data/model_results.json` | Portfolio summary | Derived | Reference metrics and test fixture |

See the existing [field dictionary](../data/Diccionario_de_Datos.md) for table definitions. The pricing input files retain the exact product order used by the elasticity matrix, and tests check that its diagonal matches the named SKU-level coefficients.

## How the data should be interpreted

- Prices and units are synthetic consumer sell-out values, not distributor invoice data.
- Own-price elasticities are regressions on synthetic observations and do not identify real market responses.
- Cross-product elasticities and substitution directions are manually specified scenario assumptions.
- The historic generator does not preserve an inventory balance across weekly observations.
- There is no evidence of real competitive response, supplier terms or promotion outcomes.

The Streamlit pricing model uses only the project's synthetic six-product dataset. No external price observations are used.
